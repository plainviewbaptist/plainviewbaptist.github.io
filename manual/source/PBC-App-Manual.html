What guests can't do

Guests see only a cleaned-up copy of event and media information, with no member names, phones, or emails.

To do more, tap Sign in / Join and wait for approval.

Guests can't see

  • The Directory
  • Prayer requests
  • Profile and family
  • Announcements
  • Feedback
  • Admin tools

Guests can't

  • Add events
  • RSVP or sign up
  • Upload photos
  • Post anything

Leaders

Who Can Do What

Everything members can do, admins and owners can do too.

Admins approve new members, but they see only a name and photo, never private details, the members list, or families.

Owners (up to 2) can do everything.

Guests (not signed in, or still waiting for approval) see only Events, Watch, and Contact.
